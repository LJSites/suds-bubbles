About the shop

A small, family-run shop on East Rowland — where suits come out crisper, hemlines come up clean, and Mr. Lee remembers your name on the second visit.

Suds & Bubbles has been Covina's go-to for full-service dry cleaning, shirt laundry and tailoring for years. The shop runs the way good neighborhood businesses always have: fair prices, careful work, and a counter where people actually talk to each other.

We handle the everyday — slacks, button-downs, blouses, the wash & fold pile that's been growing in the corner — and the once-in-a-lifetime: wedding dresses, leather jackets, suit alterations that need to fit just right.
